# Customer Support FAQ

## Password Reset

Customers who cannot sign in should use the Forgot Password option
on the sign-in page. A password reset link will be sent to the
registered email address.

If the email does not arrive, customers should check their spam folder
and confirm that they are using the correct registered email address.

If the issue continues, the support team should investigate the account.

## Refund Policy

Refund eligibility depends on the product, purchase date, and applicable
refund terms. Support agents must check the order details and current
refund policy before confirming eligibility.

Agents must not promise a refund before eligibility has been verified.

## Delivery Delays

Customers can request an update on a delayed order by providing their
order reference or support ticket number.

Agents should check the latest available order or ticket information
before giving an estimated delivery date.

If the order cannot be located or the delay requires investigation,
the case should be escalated to the appropriate support team.

## Account Security

Support agents must not ask customers to share their passwords or
one-time authentication codes.

Identity should be verified through the application's approved
verification process before account-specific information is disclosed
or account changes are made.
